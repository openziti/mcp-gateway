`Dial` resolves cross-org via can-connect authorization while `Listen` is same-org. Exposing and shaping that policy surface — who may dial whose tunnels across organizations — is future product work, deliberately out of scope for the transport swap. This is agora-side product design as much as gateway work; the gateway's part is whatever config or refusal surface the policy model ends up needing.
